Questions

Is the Student plan really cheaper?

Yes — it's priced for coursework and graduation projects. This demo doesn't verify a .edu email yet; that's on the team's to-do list.

What happens when I hit my daily scan limit?

New scans are blocked until the 24-hour window resets, and the dashboard points you to this page to upgrade.

Is this connected to a real payment processor?

Not yet. Choosing a plan updates your account instantly for demo purposes — wiring up Stripe or another processor is a good next task for the team.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, any time, from this page or your account page.
